# Preorder page

The order page collects name, a required contact email, date, quantities, one required pickup window, required acknowledgement of the $5.50 CAD per 80 g price, and optional price feedback. The email is used to contact the customer about their preorder and pickup. Ingredients, allergens and frozen-storage instructions follow the supplied product information. Cake images are labelled as illustrations. The page presents a regular preorder service, with payment and exact pickup details confirmed separately.

Client and server validation require a valid email, pickup and acknowledgement for new preorders. The customer email is saved with the order, shown as a contact link in the admin list, editable by administrators, and included in CSV exports. Migration `0003_customer_email.sql` adds a nullable column so older preorders retain their data and remain editable without an email. The database stores the unit price but does not have a separate acknowledgement column. Older responses and the admin editor continue to support blank pickup times. Retries use a unique key, totals are calculated on the server, and successful submissions return a reference. This change does not send automated email.
