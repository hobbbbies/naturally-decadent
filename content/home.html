Naturally Decadent began with a question: could a creamy, indulgent cake be made with recognizable, recognizable ingredients, without dairy or refined sugar?

Our answer is a collection of four no-bake cake concepts, imagined for dessert lovers in Guelph who want more choice. The idea is simple: a lovely slice, a flavour you love, and convenient local preorder pickup.

How much is a slice?+

The proposed introductory price is $5.50 CAD for one 80 g slice, for all four flavours: Chocolate, Lemon, Mango and Raspberry. You may also share optional feedback about the price.

Are the cakes dairy-free and vegan?+

The recipes are dairy-free and made without refined sugar. The Lemon cake contains honey and is not vegan. Ingredients and key allergens for each flavour are listed above and on the order page. Email us if you have a specific ingredient question.

How should the cakes be stored?+

Keep frozen until ready to serve. Thaw in the refrigerator (4°C or below) and consume within 3 days of thawing.

Where and when would pickup happen?+

Local pickup is proposed for Guelph, Ontario; the exact location is to be confirmed. The form asks which time would suit you if pickup were offered Monday–Sunday: 9–11 AM, 11 AM–1 PM, 1–3 PM, or 3–5 PM. Selecting a time does not book an appointment.

How do I submit a mock preorder?+

Open the order page, enter your name, date, cake quantities, and preferred hypothetical pickup window, then select “Submit mock preorder.” You can also share optional feedback about the proposed price. Your response is saved and a confirmation reference appears on the page.
